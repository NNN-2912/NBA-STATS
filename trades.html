How trades are scored

Gain is how many more of the 9 categories you would expect to win in an average week, against every team in the league, after the trade. For example, +0.30 means about one extra category won every three weeks. Each team is judged on its best 10 healthy players.

Stats: "This season + projections" mixes ESPN's season projection with this season's real numbers, trusting the real numbers more as games are played (half and half at about 10 games).

Weak categories are the ones marked "Need" above. With "Must not get worse", a trade is only shown if none of them drops by more than half a point.

Fair trades are ones where the value the other manager receives is close to what they give up, and their own expected category wins don't drop much. Only trades that help them too keeps the ones where their team also improves, which are the easiest to get accepted.

Players who are out or on IR are left out of both sides.
